Agents cannot complete a task correctly when essential context is missing. Zep is the unified context layer for enterprise data. It combines business data, documents, and conversations into shared, governed context that agents and applications can retrieve.
Zep organizes this context in temporal Context Graphs. The Context Lake manages and serves millions of these graphs as one system on Konig, Zep’s graph database service.
Enterprise context graphs
Unify knowledge about projects, products, operations, and business domains for agents across your organization.
